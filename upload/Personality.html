About Assessment

This assessment is based on personality type – a set of basic drives and motivations that remain constant throughout a person's life. The model of personality type is non-judgmental.

Assessment Output

  • Detailed Report: Four-letter personality code with strengths, challenges and recommendations.
  • Recommended Careers: Careers you are likely to succeed in based on your type.
  • Strengths & Challenges: Each type has its own inherent strengths and potential challenges.

Instructions
