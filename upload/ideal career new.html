Career Motivators

Career Motivators are the factors that make you feel happy and satisfied with your work. These include money, independence, helping others, stability, leadership, and personal interest.

Every person is motivated by different things. Choosing a career that matches your personal motivators will make you more successful and satisfied in the long run.

Examples of Motivators:

A business owner might be motivated by income and leadership.

A doctor or social worker is often motivated by helping society and gaining respect.

An artist or writer is usually motivated by freedom and creative passion.
